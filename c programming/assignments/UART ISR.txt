UART using interrupt approach

1. wap to transmitt HELLO 
2. wap to receive 5 characters(using interrupt approach) & transmitt it 
3. wap to tranmitt ASCII values of digits of following number
   int x = 543;
4. wap to receive two digits (as two bytes), combine them to form a decimal number 
   & send it on P1
   e.g  if first byte received is 0000 1001
        & second byte received is 0000 0010
        then 92(decimal)should be sent on P1
5. wap to receive 5 characters & after complete reception send them on P2 with time gap of 1 sec between every two characters
