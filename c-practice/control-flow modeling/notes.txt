Loops: Repeat this until a condition changes.

    Before choosing for, while, or do-while, ask:

        1. What changes each iteration?

        2. Who owns that changes?

        3. What makes it stop?

        FOR: When Iteration Count Is Known 

            - You know how many times
            - Or you're iterating over a fixed size
            - Or you're stepping by a fixed increment

            EX:
                - Scan 8 GPIO pins
                - Transmit 16 bytes
                - Fill 100-element buffer

        WHILE: When Condition Controls lifetime

            - Iterations are unknown
            - Loop depends on external state
            - You're waiting for something

            EX:
                - wait for button press
                - wait for ADC conversion complete
                - Run until buffer empty

        DO-WHILE: When it must run at least once

            EX:
                - Menu input
                - Retry-at -least-once operations
